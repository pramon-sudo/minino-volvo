Purpose

Minino-Pedro is a private, non-commercial application used only by its owner, Pedro Ramon, to view the status of and send remote commands to his own Volvo EX30 through his personal assistant. It is not offered to the public or to any other Volvo owner.

Data use

The application reads vehicle data (lock, doors, windows, odometer, tyres, warnings, battery and charging state, range) and sends commands (lock, unlock, climatisation, honk/flash) only when the owner asks for it. Data is used solely to answer the owner's requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored privately and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Volvo ID.

Liability

The application is provided as-is for personal use by its owner. The owner is responsible for any command sent to his own vehicle.
